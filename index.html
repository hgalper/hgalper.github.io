About

I am a Professor of Communication at the USC Annenberg School for Communication and Journalism, where I serve as Director of Doctoral Studies and Director of the Annenberg Research Network on International Communication (ARNIC).

My research focuses on the social and economic impacts of digital technologies, with particular emphasis on digital inequality, broadband policy, and information and communication technologies for development (ICT4D). I have conducted extensive research on digital divides, internet adoption, and telecommunications policy in Latin America and the United States.

Research Areas

Digital Inequality

Examining barriers to internet access and adoption, with focus on socioeconomic factors, geographic disparities, and policy interventions.

Broadband Policy

Analyzing telecommunications regulation, universal service programs, and the effectiveness of broadband subsidy initiatives.

ICT4D

Investigating the role of digital technologies in economic development, poverty reduction, and social inclusion in Latin America.

Platform Economics

Studying labor dynamics, discrimination, and geographic factors in digital platform economies and the gig economy.

Research

My research program examines the social and economic impacts of digital technologies, with a focus on understanding and addressing digital inequalities. I employ quantitative methods to analyze large-scale datasets, conduct field experiments, and evaluate policy interventions aimed at expanding broadband access and digital inclusion.

Current Projects

Measuring the Effectiveness of Digital Inclusion Approaches (MEDIA)

Co-Principal Investigator

Funded by: The Pew Trust

This multi-phase project evaluates the impact of the Affordable Connectivity Program (ACP) and other digital inclusion initiatives. We analyze program uptake, assess barriers to enrollment, and examine the effects of broadband subsidies on employment, education, and health outcomes.

Broadband Adoption Statewide Survey

Co-Principal Investigator

Funded by: California Emerging Technology Fund / California Department of Technology

Comprehensive assessment of broadband adoption and digital equity in California, examining barriers to internet access, digital literacy levels, and the effectiveness of state programs aimed at closing the digital divide.

Annenberg Research Network on International Communication (ARNIC)

Director

As Director of ARNIC, I lead a research initiative focused on international communication policy, digital inclusion, and the global diffusion of information and communication technologies. ARNIC produces policy research, convenes expert dialogues, and supports Ph.D. students working on international communication issues.

Teaching

I believe in research-informed teaching that connects theoretical frameworks to real-world policy challenges. My courses emphasize quantitative methods, critical analysis of empirical evidence, and the application of communication research to address pressing social issues.

Doctoral Mentorship

As Director of Doctoral Studies at USC Annenberg, I oversee the Ph.D. program and mentor doctoral students working on topics related to digital inequality, telecommunications policy, and international communication.
